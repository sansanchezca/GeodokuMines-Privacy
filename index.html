Privacy · Support

In short

DokuVatz Mines is a logic puzzle game for iPhone and iPad made by Santiago Sanchez (NeoSkybox). We collect nothing about you unless you choose to play online. There is no sign-in, no email and no analytics of our own. Your progress, stars, stones and settings are saved only on your device, and deleting the app deletes them.

The first time you open the game it asks which age group you are in. The answer stays on your device and is only used to choose which ads are suitable and whether online play is offered.

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: optional reward videos and one ad at the bottom of the board screen. Google's software in the app collects the data listed below to show and measure those ads.

What Google AdMob collects

  • Approximate location worked out from your IP address (city or region, never GPS).
  • Which ads you see and tap.
  • Device and app details: model, iOS version, app version, crashes and performance.

Ads are never personalized, for anyone. The game never asks to track you and never reads your device's advertising identifier. Players under 13 only see ads made for children.

Google uses this for advertising and measurement under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Online play (optional)

Online play lets you race the same board against friends or another player. It is optional, it is only offered to players 13 and older, and the rest of the game works without it. If you use it, the game makes an anonymous account on our server. It never asks for an email, a real name, a phone number or an Apple ID.

  • A random account number, and the @handle you pick. Please don't use your real name.
  • Your rating, wins and losses, and the result and times of your rated matches.
  • Your friends list and friend requests, the players you block, and the names you report.
  • A check from Apple (App Attest) that the account was made by the real game on a real device. It does not identify you or your device to us.
  • Your IP address, used for a moment to stop abuse. It is kept only as a scrambled code that changes every day and is deleted after a day.
  • Messages. There is no chat: players never send each other text, pictures or voice.
  • Your position on the board. Other players only see how many gems you have found.

Other players can see your @handle, your rating and your results. The server runs on Cloudflare, which processes this data for us and for nothing else. We never sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to track you.

The key to your account is stored in your device's Keychain (and in iCloud Keychain, if you use it, so your other devices can use the same account).

What nobody collects

  • Your name, email or phone number.
  • Your exact location.
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • Payment details. Nothing in the game is sold.
  • Your game progress and settings. They stay on your device.
  • Your daily reminder. If you turn it on, it is scheduled on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

Your choices

Tracking

There is none to turn off: the game does not track you and never shows Apple's tracking prompt.

Blocking and reporting

In online play you can block any player (they can no longer find you, add you or play with you) and report a name that should not be there. Reports are reviewed and offensive names are removed.

Ad consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states, the app asks for your consent before any ads are requested. You can change it in the game under Settings › Privacy › Change my ad consent.

Deleting your data

Your game data lives only on your device. Deleting the app, or choosing Delete all my data in Settings, erases it, together with your ad consent choice, which the app will ask again where the law requires it. We hold no copy. If you made an online account, Delete all my data also deletes it from our server at once, with your @handle, rating, results, friends, blocks and reports. If you deleted the app first, write to us and we will delete the account for you. Data that Google AdMob collected while showing ads is handled by Google under its own policies, linked above.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Players who say they are under 13 only see ads made for children, and online play is not offered to them, so no account is ever made for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas for DokuVatz Mines? Write to support@neoskybox.com, reach NeoSkybox on Instagram at @neoskybox, or open an issue here.

How to play: every color on the board hides exactly one gem; every other square is a mine. One gem per color, per row and per column, and gems never touch, not even at the corners. Tap to chalk a square you have ruled out, and hold a square to dig it. The full rules are in the game under Settings › How to play.
